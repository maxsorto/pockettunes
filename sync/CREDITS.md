## Third-party software

- **dr_mp3** by David Reid (public domain / MIT-0). Realtime MP3 decoding on the device.
- **openfpgaOS SDK** by thinkelastic. The prebuilt RISC-V soft CPU, audio/video/input APIs, and core packaging. Not redistributed here; grab it from the original source.
- **Analogue Pocket / openFPGA** by Analogue. The hardware platform this runs on.
- **Hono**, **Vite**, **React**, **TypeScript**. The Sync companion app.
- **ffmpeg / ffprobe**. Audio transcoding and album-art conversion in the Sync pipeline.
